Research Projects

AURA: Binaural Spatial Question Answering with a Frozen General-Audio Codec
Lead Project Member · Jun 2026 – Present
Advisors: Prof. Yiwei Wang

  • Developed AURA, a spatial audio understanding framework for binaural spatial question answering that reorganizes frozen general-audio codec representations to bridge audio–text information asymmetry.
  • Introduced scene-aware supervision to align audio and textual modalities, diagnosing and resolving the audio–text information asymmetry that limits spatial reasoning performance.
  • Achieved competitive spatial reasoning with approximately 40% of the trainable parameters of specialized baselines and strong zero-shot generalization to general audio tasks. Submitted to TPAMI.

ManimAgent: Self-Evolving Multimodal Agents for Visual Education
Contributing Member · Apr 2026 – Jun 2026

  • Co-developed ManimAgent, a self-evolving multimodal agent that generates Manim animation code from scientific paper sections via VLM-guided reflection.
  • Built a dual-channel episodic memory bank — positive success rationales and negative failure patterns — populated entirely from the agent's own task stream, with no weight updates and no human seeds.
  • Improved first-attempt animation generation success from 68.5% to 84.9% and reduced average reflection rounds from 12.2 to 6.5. Published at EMNLP 2026 (Main).

Training-Free Format Control for Large Diffusion Language Models
Independent Researcher · Jun 2025 – Oct 2025
Advisors: Prof. Chi Zhang, Prof. Yiwei Wang

  • Implemented a training-free inference algorithm for large diffusion language models, utilizing token-level softmax scores to guide dynamic anchor selection, ensuring robust structural integrity across diverse scenarios in zero-shot settings.
  • Conducted rigorous evaluations on mathematical reasoning (GSM8K, MATH) and JSON benchmarks against Dream-7B and static infilling baselines.
  • Significantly enhanced format adherence while preserving reasoning accuracy: achieved over 70% format retention in mathematical reasoning and maintained a stable ~80% valid JSON generation rate across various extraction methods.

Hard to Read, Easy to Jailbreak: Visual Degradation vs. MLLM Safety Alignment
Core Contributor · Oct 2025 – Feb 2026
Advisors: Prof. Yiwei Wang, Prof. Chi Zhang

  • Identified a visual-degradation-induced jailbreak vulnerability in multimodal LLMs, where lowering image resolution inadvertently bypasses safety alignment even when the text remains legible.
  • Characterized the underlying "Cognitive Overload" mechanism, showing that decoding degraded input (low resolution, noise, geometric distortion) diverts model attention away from safety auditing.
  • Proposed a Structured Cognitive Offloading defense that serializes visual transcription and safety assessment, significantly reducing jailbreak success rates while preserving visual text recognition accuracy. Published at ACL 2026 (Findings).

SQLAgent: Learning to Explore Before Generating as a Data Engineer
Contributing Member · Aug 2025 – Oct 2025
Advisors: Prof. Yiwei Wang, Prof. Chi Zhang

  • Co-developed SQLAgent, a two-stage Text-to-SQL framework that separates database knowledge acquisition from query generation.
  • Designed an exploration stage using a Monte Carlo Tree Search-inspired strategy to autonomously build a database-specific knowledge base of schema fragments, executable queries, and natural-language descriptions.
  • Built a dual-agent deployment stage for iterative retrieval and SQL generation, achieving 25.78% accuracy on Spider 2.0-Snow and improved generalization to complex, unfamiliar databases. Published at ACL 2026 (Findings).

Optimizing Diffusion LLMs via Logic-Aware Reinforcement Learning Reward Design
Lead Project Member · Nov 2025 – Jan 2026
Advisors: Prof. Chi Zhang, Prof. Yiwei Wang

  • Proposed and implemented a logic-aware RL reward design to address autoregressive degradation in complex reasoning tasks.
  • Constructed a multi-dimensional reward mechanism — encompassing parallelism, dependency, and result correctness rewards — to guide the optimization of diffusion LLMs through logical structure constraints and generation parallelism.
  • Systematically evaluated on the ListOps reasoning benchmark, exploring robustness across various positional sampling algorithms. Significantly enhanced generation quality for complex logical tasks while substantially improving parallel decoding efficiency.
